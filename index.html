How it works

Three steps. Fully automated.
No staff required.

1

Enquiry arrives

A patient contacts your practice via your website form, missed call, email, Facebook, Instagram, or Google Business Profile. Flowstate captures it instantly — even at 11pm on a Sunday.

2

AI qualifies the lead

Every enquiry is scored 0–10. The system detects urgency, extracts contact details, identifies treatment interest, and flags high-value patients — all before a human sees it.

3

Personalised reply sent

A tailored SMS or email goes out within 2 minutes — using your practice name and tone. If there's no reply, follow-ups run automatically. Patient questions are forwarded to your practice manager instantly.
